What we do

Sustainable peace is built when people come together.

At Congo Peace Academy, we know that sustainable peace is built when people come together. Our programs — spanning education, peacebuilding, entrepreneurship, and conservation agriculture (FFF) — create spaces where communities connect, collaborate, and learn from one another. Through these shared experiences, empathy, trust, and mutual respect grow, transforming conflict into an opportunity for lasting change.

Building Sustainable Peace

Communities able to resolve conflict through dialogue, not violence.

By engaging people across generations and sectors, our programs foster collaboration and shared responsibility. Youth become proactive leaders, farmers secure stable livelihoods, and communities learn to resolve conflicts through dialogue rather than violence. Together, these efforts transform isolated initiatives into community-wide resilience, ensuring that peace is not temporary, but deeply rooted and enduring.
